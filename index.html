Learn the words for what you already do

Armenia teaches chess as a compulsory school subject from the age of six — the only country in the world that does. Vocabulary sticks when it is attached to something you actually care about, so pick a domain you already know well and learn its words first. Chess, cooking, music, hiking: the subject matters less than the fact that you have opinions about it.

Շախմատ
shakhmat — chess

Tatik yev Papik — "Grandma and Grandpa"

This is a woven version of "We Are Our Mountains," the basalt monument outside Stepanakert — two enormous carved heads emerging from the mountain, known affectionately by this name. Rugs like this one turn a landmark into something a household keeps and hands down, the same way this app is trying to turn an alphabet into something you keep.

Մենք ենք մեր սարերը
Menk' yenk' mer sarery — We are our mountains

Tip: The Armenian alphabet is phonetic — once you know the letters, you can read anything. Invest your first 2 weeks entirely in alphabet mastery before worrying about grammar or vocabulary. Handwriting each letter (not just typing) dramatically improves retention.
